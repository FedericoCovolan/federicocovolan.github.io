04 / Cookie Policy

This website uses only technical cookies necessary for its correct operation. No profiling cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party marketing cookies are used.

Technical cookies are essential for the functioning of the website and do not require consent. They include session cookies that are automatically deleted when the browser is closed.

This website does not use Google Analytics, advertising cookies, or any tracking systems that monitor user behaviour across different websites.
