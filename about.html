FEDERICO COVOLAN

Systemic and strategic designer

Federico Covolan is a Systemic and Strategic Designer with a multidisciplinary background shaped by my studies at the Politecnico di Torino, the Oslo School of Architecture (AHO) and the Università di Ferrara.

My practice is rooted in the ability to visualize, prototype, and transform complexity into circular opportunities through strategic research and system thinking. As a Duckweed enthusiast and the founder of ProLemna, I am dedicated to shaping the future of sustainable agrifood systems, implementing Duckweed potential to revolutionize the supply chain through systemic innovation.

I am driven by a passion for continuous learning and discovery, whether I am experimenting with new ways to visualize complexity or exploring the outdoors. Outside of design, I find my balance in nature: hiking, walking, and staying active at the gym or diving into a good book. I am always eager to apply systemic tools to create meaningful, lasting impact.

Driven by a passion for continuous learning and discovery, my work has evolved into a deeply multidisciplinary practice focused on navigating the layers of contemporary complexity. I tackle innovation through a Systemic lens, treating every challenge not as a standalone product, but as a living, breathing ecosystem.

My expertise spans across Product design, Service orchestration, and Speculative futures, with a core focus on making invisible connections both visible and functional. Drawing from my background at Politecnico di Torino and AHO Oslo, I specialize in translating complex socio-technical data into clear, strategic narratives.

My goal is to bridge the gap between high-level strategy and the modular, practical execution required to scale a vision.

I am committed to a design practice that is critical, sustainable, and accessible. Whether I am analyzing deep-tech research, papers, or patents, I strive to turn technical insights into human-centered experiences.

PRO LEMNA STARTUP

Starting as a bachelor thesis project, ProLemna won the Range Rover Leadership Academy in 2024 and began its journey as a startup and it was subsequently incubated at 2i3T. As founder and Business Developer, I translate the systemic approach into operational management, coordinating the strategic vision to ensure the circularity of the model and taking care of business development through business plans and financial analysis.

I also manage stakeholder relations, handling relationships with investors and partners through pitching, and oversee project management, guiding a multidisciplinary team towards market entry.
