Systemic, Industrial
and Social Design

A collection of works exploring the transition from tangible objects to complex, intangible systems. Since 2022, my practice has evolved from Product Design to Systemic and Strategic frameworks, utilizing Speculative and Service tools to tackle contemporary challenges.

Each project is an exercise in visualizing the invisible connections that make an ecosystem work, moving from high-level research to practical, modular execution.
