Product Design · 2022

LEAVES WATER
SYSTEM

Fence and Rainwater Collector

Students Agnese Baldoni
Federico Covolan
Coordinating Teachers Giuseppe Mincolelli
Lucia Botti
Michele Marchi
Project Length 5 months
My Role Research and analysis, concept design, modelling, rendering, technical drawing, ergonomic aspects, prototyping
Project Place Università di Ferrara
Villa Smeraldi – Parco di Bentivoglio

Leaves Water System is the project of a modular and multifunctional structure for outdoors, with a shape linked to the natural environment in which it was conceived, the park of the province of Bologna, at Villa Smeraldi.

The fence, in addition to having the function of delimitation, has a mechanism for watering the plants that grow around it. Rainwater is collected through the basin placed as a cover of the agricultural equipment, it is collected in a water barrel and then channeled into a pipe along the perimeter of the fence. Through valves the water is distributed to the plants which receive the water they need to grow.

The peculiarity of the structure is that, since it does not reach a high weight, it does not need bases or plinths in the ground but the structure is supported by a steel base on which the plant pots are placed, anchored to the ground through some pickets, which together with the weight of the base, they balance the column and allow it not to sink into the ground.

Park System Layout

After some inspections we analyzed what were the critical points of the park and the area in which we concentrated is that of the absence and state of abandonment of the agricultural equipment of the Museum of Rural Civilization present in the Villa of the park.

In the park there are fencing systems from the 80s made up of stakes planted in the ground and plastic strings, these fences are dangerous for children who can access the agricultural equipment, they do not protect the equipment from free animals in the park and do not intrigue the visitors.

The intent is therefore to protect the equipment and at the same time enhance it, give a new life to these objects that carry the history of the park with them.

Construction Details & Prototype

Various materials were used to create the model, such as clay to model the tubular, paper mache and cardboard to create the fence, a breathable canvas was used for the fabric and a more robust fabric for the central part which must hold rain water.

The metal tubulars were made with an iron wire, finally the parts were colored using acrylic colors and a spray can. The structure is attached to the ground, made with a 2 cm thick section of wood, which was then dusted over a green powder similar to grass and soil.
