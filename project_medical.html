Systemic and Strategic Design · 2025

PROGETTI
MEDICAL

Defibrillators in residential buildings: a strategy to fight cardiac arrest

Students Angelo Angiola, Federico Bottari
Nicolò Buselli, Giulia Campione
Federico Covolan, Sila Yasar
Tito Poles, Silin Zhu
Coordinating Teachers Silvia Barbero, Comino Elena
Beppe Pedone, Peruccio Pier Paolo
Project Length 5 months
My Role Sketching, systemic design research, sustainability strategy, development of strategic scenarios, maps and datasheet
Project Place Politecnico di Torino · 2025

This project, conducted for the Open Systems course at Politecnico di Torino in collaboration with Exclusive Brands Torino (EBT), focuses on the systemic analysis of Progetti Medical to enhance the quality of relationships within the network.

By applying a Systemic Design approach, the research moves beyond individual product logic to implement a Holistic Diagnosis of the enterprise's current state, identifying hidden resources and potential circularities within the high-end medical sector.

Progetti is the main defibrillators manufacturer in Italy — a B2B company founded in 1990 with worldwide exporting.

Intervention Strategy

Through the activation of cross-sectoral synergies within the EBT ecosystem, the project proposes a Systemic Model that fosters local development and environmental resilience.

The final strategy identified 4 areas of intervention: Awareness (rebranding, omnichannel strategy), B2C market expansion (new defibrillator for domestic use), Service (refurbishment and rental model), and B2B market development.

B2C Rental Model

The rental model includes a flexible B2C rental model at €24.99/month over an 8-year life cycle, making defibrillators accessible to individuals without upfront costs.

The regeneration process is optimal for low innovation devices such as the AED, ensuring up to 30% reduction in material use and 40% decrease in final cost — making the model both environmentally and economically sustainable.
