PROGETTI MEDICAL

Defibrillators in residential buildings: a strategy to fight cardiac arrest

This project, conducted for the Open Systems course at Politecnico di Torino in collaboration with Exclusive Brands Torino (EBT), focuses on the systemic analysis of Progetti Medical to enhance the quality of relationships within the network.

By applying a Systemic Design approach, the research moves beyond individual product logic to implement a Holistic Diagnosis of the enterprise's current state, identifying hidden resources and potential circularities within the high-end medical sector.

The study utilizes Open Systems principles to map flushes of matter, energy, and information, transforming traditional waste into new opportunities for value creation and strengthening the bond between medical innovation and the Piedmontese territory.

Progetti is the main defibrillators manufacturer in Italy — a B2B company, founded in 1990, with worldwide exporting.

Diving into Research

Through the activation of cross-sectoral synergies within the EBT ecosystem, the project proposes a Systemic Model that fosters local development and environmental resilience, ensuring that the excellence of Progetti Medical contributes to a self-sustaining and interconnected business habitat.

The research identified +22 challenges, +46 opportunities, and 1 final strategy — leading to 4 areas of intervention.

4 Areas of Intervention

1. Awareness — From rational to emotional. While B2B market focuses on technical aspects, B2C market focuses on emotional aspects. Rebranding to give consistency to Progetti Medical's core values and identity. Omnichannel strategy to create awareness on cardioprotection through different types of actions.

2. B2C — New defibrillator suitable for a domestic environment. Humanity Centered Design method. Improve accessibility and usability using the principles of ergonomics, usability and cognitive psychology.

3. Service — Refurbishment: the rental model includes the components and defibrillator refurbishment. Close to users: once the long-term renting is ended Progetti can easily reach the defibrillator and change it. Sustainability: the regeneration is optimal for low innovation devices such as the AED, ensuring up to 30% reduction in material use and 40% decrease in final cost.

4. B2B — New markets: keep trying to enter new markets, such as the Chinese and American ones. Staff implementation: dedicating a section for the abroad export components and devices + partners relationships.

B2C Rental Model & Financial Impact

The B2C Rental Model offers a flexible approach at €24.99/month over a life cycle of 8 years, making defibrillators accessible to individuals and residential buildings without upfront costs.

The rental model ensures steady revenues and gradually less production over time. In Turin alone, there are 323,823 residential buildings, with approximately 32,382 condominiums representing a 12% market target for the pilot launch.

The 4% annual growth forecast is based on company performance from 2018 to 2023, excluding the COVID sales spike. Net profit grows with sales but stays flat in year one due to investments in a new defibrillator and awareness campaigns.

Redesign of the Progetti Medical Company Process

Company Map 2.0 represents the strategic evolution of the Progetti Medical ecosystem. Building on the Holistic Diagnosis, this map outlines the transition from a linear model to an open and resilient system. By integrating 'hidden resources' and designing new circular flows of materials, energy and information, the map visualises a network of interconnected businesses.

The result is an industrial ecosystem where technological excellence in the biomedical sector acts as a driving force for regional development and systemic sustainability.
