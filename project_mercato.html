IL MERCATO DELLE STORIE

Social innovation project enhancing creativity in Turin's Aurora neighbourhood

Aurora is a neighbourhood within the 7th District of Turin, situated close to the city centre but characterised by its demographic and socioeconomic features that already classify it as a periphery. The research aims to explore the concept of creativity in its broadest and most complex sense, particularly focusing on how it can be directed towards young creative individuals within the cultural context of the Torino Creativa project.

To investigate the neighbourhood, we employed a flexible method that combines territorial analysis tools, gathering and analysing both statistical-quantitative and perceptual-qualitative information. Starting with a historical overview of the Aurora district, we carefully considered factors related to the population composition, which is notably multicultural and youthful.

Aurora, due to its diverse population, stands out as one of the liveliest and most dynamic neighbourhoods in the Piedmontese capital. It is a place of contradictions: bustling with commerce, abundant in services for vulnerable groups, yet with public spaces that need attention.

"The Marketplace of Stories" is a systemic, organic, multi-initiative project with the ultimate goal of enhancing the creativity present in the Aurora neighbourhood by telling it. The project is developed with the podcast "Dimmi Tutto" (Tell Me Everything), the market stall (Bancarella), and an annual festival.

Creativity is narrated with the common thread of the market, the beating heart and symbol of the Aurora neighbourhood, through different project outputs such as interviews, meetings and works. The creatives involved are rare, little-known creatives who deserve a space to tell their story and present their vision of "creativity." The Story Market is about territory, participation and exchange.

Communication Strategy
